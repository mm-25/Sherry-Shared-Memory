SHERRY

Privacy notice

Updated September 30, 2026

Sherry Capture is a private browser extension that copies supported AI conversations into your Sherry workspace so you can retrieve context across AI providers.

What the extension handles

  • Visible user messages and AI responses on supported ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini pages.
  • Conversation titles, page URLs, message order, timestamps, and links.
  • Attachment names and metadata when attachment capture is enabled.
  • Your Memory account email and authentication session.
  • Sensitive information—including health or financial information—when it appears in a captured conversation.
  • Limited technical information only when Firefox technical-data permission is enabled.

Deleted messages and temporary chats are not intentionally collected by default. Attachment capture is off by default.

Why the data is used

Captured information is used to provide your Sherry workspace: conversation storage, summaries, Personal Memory notes, retrieval, source attribution, synchronization, diagnostics, deletion, and export. Sherry no longer creates new automatic memory claims.

Where data is stored

The extension sends captured data over HTTPS to the Memory ingestion service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Raw archives are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. Structured workspace data and account authentication are hosted in Supabase.

Sherry looks for common password, API-key, private-key, payment-card, and labelled-credential patterns and replaces matches before durable storage. This automatic check may miss unfamiliar formats. Your Sherry account password is sent directly to Supabase for authentication and is not saved by the extension. Session tokens remain in your browser profile and are excluded from diagnostic exports.

Your controls

You can pause capture, control attachment and temporary-chat capture, disconnect cloud synchronization, inspect source conversations, export chats, summaries, and Personal Memory notes, and delete individual stored conversations. The Financial, Health, and Personal switches keep stored information in Sherry but prevent connected AI tools from retrieving disabled categories. When any switch is off, Sherry also withholds unlabelled chats and summaries from connected AI tools. Disconnecting synchronization does not delete data already stored in Sherry.

Deleting one conversation removes it from Sherry's transcript and search and cleans up raw archive copies. If archive cleanup is delayed, Sherry retries it in the background. The original chat remains with its AI provider. Deleting an entire Sherry workspace or account is not yet available in this private prototype.

Sharing and retention

Sherry does not sell captured data or use it for advertising. Cloudflare, Supabase, and a user-selected summarization provider process data as needed to provide the configured service. Captured data remains until you delete it; there is currently no automatic workspace retention setting.

Prototype status

This is an unlisted prototype for private testing. Its privacy controls and storage design will be reviewed again before any public release.
