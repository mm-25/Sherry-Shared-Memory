Sherry removes these chats and their summaries and cleans up raw archive copies. Your account, Personal Memory notes, settings, and AI connections stay. Original chats at AI providers stay there, but Sherry will block these same chats from being captured or imported again. This cannot be undone. Keep this page open until deletion finishes.
