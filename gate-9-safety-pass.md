## What deleting one chat means

When the user deletes a conversation in Sherry, the Worker removes that conversation's messages and search records, deletes or rewrites the raw R2 archives that contain it, and records a tombstone for its workspace, provider, and provider conversation ID. Later extension scans or imports that submit **that same provider conversation ID** are ignored, so a deleted chat does not quietly reappear in Sherry. This is what “prevents recapture” means. A new provider conversation with a different ID is a separate source, even if its words are similar. The original chat in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini is not deleted by Sherry. Independent notes are not deleted just because a conversation is deleted; full workspace deletion is not implemented yet.

The existing live deletion path can leave historical derived claims behind because its conversation and checkpoint foreign keys use `SET NULL`. Nine source-less rows remain from the earlier claim feature: all nine are provisional, have no conversation link, evidence row, or checkpoint reference, and are not used by current retrieval. Two rows repeat the same Gemini-testing statement. The available database evidence does not prove which deletion, if any, caused each row to lose its source. The prepared migration removes only source-less derived claims without evidence and makes future conversation deletion remove claims linked to the deleted conversation. The other 1,882 conversation-linked historical claims are not deleted by this one-time cleanup. This production cleanup has not run.
